What Makes Frozen Seafood and Steaks Good Options for Noche Buena?

Frozen seafood and steaks give home cooks a way to shop earlier and thaw on their own schedule, instead of leaving every holiday choice until the last minute.

A mix of tuna, salmon, and steak also gives you flexibility. You can plan a seafood starter, a baked or grilled main dish, and a steak option if you want beef on the table as well. Choose products that fit the recipes you already have in mind, then follow the storage and preparation instructions on each pack.

What Tuna Dishes Can You Prepare for Noche Buena?

Tuna works well for a holiday meal because different cuts suit different cooking styles, from grilled mains to a Japanese-style plate.

How Can You Prepare Tuna Panga for Christmas?

Tuna panga refers to the head and jaw area of the tuna. It is a cut many home cooks grill or bake for a shared main dish. The Menu lists:

  • Tuna Panga (Regular)
  • Tuna Panga Yellowfin
  • Tuna Panga Bigeye

Season it with your preferred marinade, then grill or bake it and serve it as part of the main meal.

What Can You Make with Tuna Belly?

Tuna belly is another cut to consider when you want a richer grilled or baked seafood dish. We carry Tuna Belly Yellowfin. You can cook it with your preferred seasoning and serve it with rice, vegetables, or the sides you already planned for Noche Buena.

Can You Include Tuna Sashimi on Your Christmas Table?

If you enjoy Japanese-style dishes, a sashimi plate can add variety to the holiday spread. Tuna Sashimi (200g - Sliced) is a pre-sliced pack, so you do not need to cut a block yourself. Arrange the slices on a clean plate and pair them with wasabi and sushi soy sauce, which are available as sushi add-ons.

Sashimi is a Japanese-style way of serving sliced fish without cooking. The phrase sashimi-grade is commonly used in seafood retail, but it is not a universal certification or a guarantee that a product is safe to eat raw. Confirm that the specific product is suitable for that use, and follow its handling and storage instructions.

For more on choosing tuna for sashimi at home, see our guide Where to Buy Sashimi-Grade Tuna in Iloilo.

What Simple Starter Can You Add to Your Christmas Menu?

Not every dish needs an elaborate preparation. Consider Tuna Lumpia (36pcs) as a simple starter for your Noche Buena spread. Lumpia is a familiar Filipino-style food, and this version uses tuna. Serve it with your preferred dipping sauce alongside the other dishes you have planned.

How Should You Plan Ahead and Thaw Holiday Seafood?

Buying frozen seafood and steaks lets you plan earlier, but storage and handling still matter.

Here are some general reminders:

  • Review your planned recipes before you decide which products and pack sizes to buy.
  • Keep packs frozen until you are ready to use them, and follow the storage instructions on each product.
  • Thaw in the refrigerator, and keep seafood and meat cold during preparation.
  • Use clean utensils, knives, cutting boards, and serving plates.
  • Follow the instructions on the pack, along with official food-safety advice.

If you plan to serve sashimi, make sure the particular product is suitable for raw consumption. Freezing or a product name alone should not be treated as a guarantee of safety.
