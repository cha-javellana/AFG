How to thaw and prepare

General steps for frozen seafood at home. Follow the instructions on your pack as well.

  • Keep packs frozen until you're ready to use them.
  • Best method: thaw in the refrigerator overnight, or for several hours.
  • Quicker method: put the sealed pack in a bowl of cold water and change the water every 30 minutes. Never use hot water or leave it at room temperature.
  • For vacuum-sealed packs, open or loosen the seal before thawing in the fridge. This follows FDA advice for reduced-oxygen packaging.
  • For sashimi and saku: slice while still slightly firm with a sharp knife, serve chilled, and eat the same day.
  • For steaks: pat dry, season and sear. FDA's safe cooking temperature for fish is 63°C (145°F) if you prefer it fully cooked.
  • Don't refreeze thawed seafood. Use it within a day.

These steps are based on the US FDA page, Selecting and Serving Fresh and Frozen Seafood Safely. For tuna sashimi at home, see Where to Buy Sashimi-Grade Tuna in Iloilo.
